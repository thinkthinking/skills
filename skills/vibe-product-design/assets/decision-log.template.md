# Decision Log — [Product name]

> Append-only. Every real product decision, why it was made, and what was
> rejected. The rejected alternatives are often the most valuable part — they
> stop the team from re-litigating settled questions. Log decisions, not status
> updates.

## YYYY-MM-DD · [Decision title]
- **Decision:** what we chose.
- **Context:** the question or tension that forced a choice.
- **Reasoning:** why this option won (tie to a layer or a thinking lens).
- **Rejected:** the alternatives considered, and why each lost.
- **Revisit if:** the condition under which we should reopen this.
